# vettr brand kit

Logo, wordmark, icons and usage rules. The mark is the **Delta Square**: a square and its shifted copy,
with only the change inked. Ink is what was, green is what is now. It is the product in one shape: show me
what changed.

The name is always written lowercase: **vettr** (pronounced "vetter": to vet a change is to check it
carefully).

## Files

| Folder | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `masters/` | Source SVGs (generated by `build_logo.py`): symbol, small-size symbol, horizontal and stacked lockups, wordmark, each with an `-on-dark` version; app icon, full-bleed app icon, favicon |
| `exports/` | One-colour SVGs (`-black`, `-white`) of the symbol, both lockups and the wordmark |
| `png/` | Transparent PNGs of everything above (symbol 1024 px wide, others 2400 px wide) |
| `icons/` | App icon PNGs (16 to 1024), `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `maskable-512.png` |
| `presentation/` | Brand board (`vettr-brand-board.html`, open in a browser), its spec and slide PNGs |

Regenerate everything: `python3 branding/build_logo.py && branding/build_assets.sh` (the second step needs
ImageMagick with librsvg). All logo files are final outlines: no live text, no raster images, no filters.

## Which file to use

| Situation | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Light background (white, paper, light grey) | `vettr-horizontal.svg`, `-stacked.svg` or `-symbol.svg` (ink + green) |
| Dark background (ink, dark UI, terminal) | the `-on-dark` version (paper + light green) |
| Photo, pattern, or any colour that is not white, paper or ink (including brand green) | `-black` or `-white`, whichever contrasts |
| One-colour print, embossing, stamps, fax | `-black` (or `-white` reversed) |
| Square slot: avatar, favicon, app icon | `icons/` (dark tile) or `-symbol.svg` |
| Under 32 px | `vettr-symbol-small.svg` (thicker bands, no gap between the Ls) |

Never put the two-colour light version on a dark background: the ink L disappears. Never put the green on a
green background.

## Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#12141A` | 18 20 26 | Wordmark, the "was" L, dark surfaces |
| Signal green | `#12A474` | 18 164 116 | The "is" L, the crossbar through the two t's, accents on light |
| Paper | `#F4F5F7` | 244 245 247 | Wordmark and "was" L on dark |
| Signal green (on dark) | `#3DDC97` | 61 220 151 | The "is" L and the t-crossbar on dark |

- Ink on white is roughly 18:1. Signal green on white is about 3.1:1: fine for the symbol and other graphics
  (3:1), but **not for body text**. Signal green on dark is about 10:1.
- Two colours at most. No gradients, shadows or glows.
- CMYK and Pantone values are not assigned. Convert from the HEX values and proof on the real stock before a
  print run.

## Construction, clear space and size

- The symbol is two 144-unit rounded squares (corner radius 24) offset by one band, 32 units, down and right.
  Where the two Ls meet, the corners are rounded (radius 8) so they never touch at a point.
- **Clear space:** keep at least one band width (about 18% of the symbol's height) free on every side of any
  logo file. Do not crowd it with other marks, text or edges.
- **Minimum size:** symbol 16 px (use the small cut below 32 px) or 5 mm in print. Horizontal lockup 100 px or
  25 mm wide. Stacked lockup 64 px wide. Below that, use the symbol alone.
- **Lockups:** the horizontal lockup centres the wordmark's ink box on the symbol and leaves a gap of about
  one third of the symbol's height. Do not re-space it; use the files.

## Misuse

Do not: recolour the Ls, swap their colours or order, rotate or mirror the mark, stretch it, outline it, add
effects, put it in a container shape other than the supplied app icon tile, retype the name in a font, change
the case of "vettr", recolour or move the t-crossbar, or draw your own lockup.

## Wordmark

Custom lowercase geometric letters built from circles, rectangles and two diagonals: x-height 100 units,
straight stems 22, round strokes 23. The two t's share a single crossbar, drawn in signal green: it echoes the
"added" L of the symbol and reads like a redline through the word. The `t` stems are the tallest parts of the
word, the `e` has an open aperture and the `r` a plain arm, so it stays legible when small. No licensed font is
involved, so there are no font licence limits.

## What was tested

- `svg_audit.py` on every master: single fills, no text, no strokes, integer canvases. The only note is on the wordmark: the `e`'s terminal is a radial cut at 48 degrees, which is intentional on a curved letter.
- Preview sheet on the symbol and small cut: 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 48 px ladder and pixel test, one-colour black
  and white, greyscale, squint blur, mirrored, rotated 180 degrees, light / dark / brand / photo / pattern
  backgrounds. Readings stay clean; the findings above (dark and green backgrounds) are why the on-dark and
  one-colour versions exist.
- Brand board mockups: README, app icon, website header, terminal, sticker, social profile.

## Open items

- **The name "vettr" has not had a trademark search.** The previous working name, "agentide", clashed with two
  existing AgentIDE projects and was dropped. A web search found no developer tool called vettr, but one
  unverified concern remains: a Canadian stock-research company with a similar name. Search the WIPO Global
  Brand Database and the USPTO / EUIPO registers for VETTR in software classes 9 and 42 and check domains
  before launch.
- **Logo similarity: only a text-based check was possible.** A web search and the skill's library found no
  matching mark, but neither can compare images. A reverse image search (Google Lens, TinEye), the WIPO Global
  Brand Database and the USPTO / EUIPO trademark searches still need to be run by a person, ideally a lawyer.
- Print proofing and CMYK / Pantone matching.
- Letter spacing was tuned by eye on screens; a human designer should review it at large display sizes.
- The small cut's gap-less Ls and the favicon were checked at 16 / 32 / 48 px renders only, not in real
  browser tabs.
- Windows `.ico` / macOS `.icns` app icons are not generated yet, and packaging does not use the icon yet (the
  Electron window does; see the README to-do).
